## Description

### Precipitation (mm)

Mean annual precipitation for the 1981–2020 historical period, derived from the University of Washington Climate Impacts Group (CIG) WRF-NARR historical simulation.

CIG ran the Weather Research and Forecasting (WRF) regional climate model, driven by the North American Regional Reanalysis (NARR), to produce gridded hourly precipitation across the Puget Sound region. The simulated precipitation was bias-corrected against observations.

Mean annual precipitation was calculated for each grid cell over the 1981–2020 period. This layer is the historical baseline for the GCM ensemble precipitation and runoff layers.

### Precipitation (mm) GCM Ensemble 2010-2040

Mean annual precipitation for the 2010–2040 climate normal period, derived from the University of Washington Climate Impacts Group (CIG) ensemble of downscaled global climate model (GCM) projections.

CIG dynamically downscaled a set of GCMs with the Weather Research and Forecasting (WRF) regional climate model and bias-corrected the results, producing gridded hourly precipitation on the same grid as the historical WRF-NARR simulation. The ensemble value is the mean of the annual precipitation totals across the ensemble members.

Mean annual precipitation was calculated for each grid cell over the 2010–2040 period. The period includes 2010 and runs through 2039; the end year is exclusive. Compare with the historical (1981–2020) precipitation layer to see the projected change.
