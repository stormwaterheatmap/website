## Description

### Runoff (mm)

Mean annual runoff for the 1981–2020 historical period. Continuous hydrologic simulation was driven by the University of Washington Climate Impacts Group (CIG) gridded, bias-corrected WRF-NARR historical precipitation (Mauger and Won 2024); see the Precipitation (mm) layer for details on the forcing data. Potential evapotranspiration (PET) inputs are gridded monthly mean values from TerraClimate ([Abatzoglou et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2017.191)).

### Runoff (mm) GCM Ensemble 2010-2040

Mean annual runoff for the 2010–2040 climate normal period. The period includes 2010 and runs through 2039; the end year is exclusive. Continuous hydrologic simulation was driven by each of the 11 University of Washington Climate Impacts Group (CIG) bias-corrected GCM precipitation projections (Mauger and Won 2024); see the Precipitation (mm) GCM Ensemble 2010–2040 layer for details on the forcing data. Total annual runoff was calculated for each GCM and year, averaged across the 11 GCMs for each year (the ensemble mean), and the yearly ensemble means were averaged over the period. Land cover, soils, slope, and the monthly potential evapotranspiration (PET) inputs from TerraClimate ([Abatzoglou et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2017.191)) are the same as for the historical runoff layer, so differences from that layer reflect the change in precipitation alone. The projections do not account for future changes in evapotranspiration.
