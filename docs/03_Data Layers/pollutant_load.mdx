## Description

Pollutant loads are the predicted mean annual mass of each constituent carried by stormwater runoff, per unit area. Each load layer is the product of a pollutant concentration layer and the mean annual runoff for the 1981–2020 historical period. One millimeter of runoff over one square meter is one liter, so concentration (mass per liter) times runoff depth (mm per year) gives mass per square meter per year. The result is divided by 1000 to move the mass up one unit: Total Suspended Solids is in g/m²/year, and Total Copper, Total Kjeldahl Nitrogen, Total Phosphorus and Total Zinc are in mg/m²/year.

A load is a rate per unit area, so pixel size does not affect it. To get the total load for a region, multiply the mean load over the region by its area.

For the inputs, see the [Pollutant Concentration](/docs/Data%20Layers/pollutant_concentration) and [Runoff](/docs/Data%20Layers/runoff) layers.
